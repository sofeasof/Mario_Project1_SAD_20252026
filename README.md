# Mario_Project1_SAD_20252026

Our project, *Trackify*, is an assignment tracking system designed to help students manage their assignments and group projects more effectively. It provides a centralized platform for organizing tasks, tracking progress, monitoring deadlines, and improving collaboration among group members. *Trackif*y aims to help students stay organized, manage their time efficiently, and reduce the risk of missing important deadline. It also aims to support students in achieving their academic goals while reducing stress and improving overall task management.
